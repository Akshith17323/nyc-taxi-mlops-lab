# S10: teardown (Day 2, 18:15–19:00)

**Goal:** nothing of this lab costs anything after you leave. Everything Terraform made is destroyed, the state bucket is gone, and `scripts/verify-teardown.sh` prints nothing.

## Recall check (5 minutes, no agent)

1. Which resources here would keep billing if you only stopped them?
2. Why does Terraform state live in a bucket Terraform didn't create, and what does that mean at teardown?

## Given

- `scripts/verify-teardown.sh`: read-only. One line for every billable thing left in us-east-1 and us-west-2 (instances, Elastic IPs, volumes, snapshots, NAT gateways, load balancers, RDS, Redshift, SageMaker notebooks, secrets, `lab-` buckets). It prints nothing and exits 0 when the account is clean.
- `.github/workflows/destroy.yaml`, on a GitHub-hosted runner with this session's credentials. It runs `terraform destroy`, empties and deletes the state bucket, then runs `verify-teardown.sh` and keeps the output as the `teardown` artifact. It needs the repository's name as confirmation, and your approval in `production`.

## Do

1. `scripts/verify-teardown.sh`: see everything that exists now.
2. `scripts/gh-secrets.sh` (the only time this lab puts AWS credentials in GitHub), then `gh workflow run destroy.yaml -f confirm=<your repo's name>`, and approve it.
3. When it's done: `scripts/verify-teardown.sh` again, from your laptop. If anything's left, it says what. Delete it by hand and say in your retro why Terraform didn't.
4. `gh secret delete AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `AWS_SESSION_TOKEN`: the session is ending, and they shouldn't outlive it.

## Done when

- `scripts/verify-teardown.sh` prints nothing, and the destroy workflow's `teardown` artifact is empty.
- Tomorrow, once Learner Lab's spend display has caught up: a screenshot of your remaining budget.
