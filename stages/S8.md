# S8: gameday (Day 2, 15:15–17:45)

**Goal:** things go wrong on purpose, one after another, and you decide what to do about each, with evidence, in a record. Finding the cause is the easy part (your agent can do it). The judgment is yours: AGENTS.md says "diagnose, don't decide". The first event (E6) arrives as S8 starts and needs a canary: start its retrain, then build the canary while it runs.

## Recall check (5 minutes, no agent)

1. Canary or blue-green for a new model: what does each protect you from?
2. What is the population stability index measuring, and what doesn't it tell you?
3. A data file fails a blocking check. Name two responses and what each costs.
4. Your new model is more accurate and slower. Who decides whether that's acceptable, and with what numbers?

## Given

- **`drift_report[month]`** (pipelines): the population stability index of pickup hour, day, zone and duration between each new month and the champion's training window. `drift_within_threshold` warns over 0.2. It runs on its own whenever a month's features land.
- **`deploy/canary.sh`:** reloads `serving-challenger` with whatever the `challenger` alias points at now, moves serving traffic to `serving-challenger` with `deploy/web.sh serving-weights`, queries Grafana Cloud for both aliases' latency and errors at each step, and puts everything back on the champion when your rule says abort. The steps and the rule are yours.
- **`.github/workflows/canary.yaml`:** steady synthetic rider traffic (`deploy/traffic.py`), your canary, the result as an artifact (`canary-result`). Dispatch it: `gh workflow run canary.yaml`.
- **The events**, fired by the instructor at the times below. Watch Dagster, Grafana and your status workflow; don't wait to be told what broke.

## Build first (30 min)

**The canary:** in `deploy/canary.sh`, set `STEPS` (the challenger's share at each step), `HOLD_SECONDS`, and `should_abort`. Use the metrics in `contracts/metrics.yaml`; a step should hold long enough for Grafana Cloud to have seen it (about a minute of lag). Decide whether your rule is relative to the champion, absolute, or both, and what it does about errors versus latency. After a canary that holds, promotion is still `promote.yaml` (approval-gated); afterwards, traffic goes back to 100/0.
